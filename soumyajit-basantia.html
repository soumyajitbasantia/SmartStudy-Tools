Soumyajit Basantia

Soumyajit Basantia is the creator of SmartStudy Tools. He built this free, fast, and mobile-friendly collection of calculators and study utilities to help students finish homework faster, plan their studies, and practice core subjects — all in one simple place.

SmartStudy Tools includes 14 tools spanning math, percentages, loans, age, unit conversion, multiplication tables, GPA/CGPA, word counting, the Pomodoro technique, BMI, marks, LCM/HCF, and fractions.
